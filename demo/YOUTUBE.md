**Title**
CostLine — two acceptance letters, same degree, $3,834 apart (Hack Atlantic 2026 demo)

**Description**
Two acceptance letters. Same degree. $3,834 apart.

CostLine is a first-year cost-of-living explorer for the 10 biggest
student cities in Atlantic Canada. Map a city, line up head-to-head
comparisons, then budget your year — every figure is cited (CMHC rental
market survey, MPHEC tuition tables, university cost guides, transit
authority fare pages) and labelled Official or Estimate. Estimates are
modeled figures — treat as ±15%.

Live app: https://sharonbasovich.github.io/costline/
Source:   https://github.com/sharonbasovich/costline

Built solo during Hack Atlantic 2026 (Sept 26–27) by Sharon Basovich,
pair-programmed with Devin (AI). All figures are planning estimates,
not financial advice.
